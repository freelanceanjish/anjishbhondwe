SCRIPT (spoken)

Hello. My name is Pooja Awasare. I am applying for the PhD position in Mechanics of Materials and Constructions at Vrije Universiteit Brussel, in the Mechanics of Advanced Materials and Structures group.

I am a civil and environmental engineer. I completed my Diploma and Bachelor’s in Civil Engineering in India, and then a Master’s in Environmental Engineering at HTW Dresden in Germany. During my civil studies I spent a lot of time in the laboratory: concrete and cube strength tests, aggregates and sand grading, steel tensile testing, and soil tests such as Proctor compaction and Atterberg limits. Those labs stayed with me. They showed me that good construction depends on how materials behave, how carefully we test them, and how we design structures that use materials well.

At HTW Dresden I continued with construction materials, a geotechnical project, and water, air and soil analysis with laboratory practicals. My master’s thesis looked at agile project management in construction, with a focus on collaboration, risk and careful use of resources. After that work I became more interested in materials performance, and in connecting mechanical behaviour with sustainability.

Professionally I have worked in Belgium, including with a construction partner on circular practices and waste-management workflows in project delivery. I want to build my research career in Belgium, and I am ready to relocate to Brussels for a start in October 2026.

I am applying to MeMC because I want to return to materials and structures at research level: advanced characterisation, mechanics-driven design, and high-performance or more sustainable construction materials. I am an early-career researcher and I am willing to prepare applications for external fellowships such as FWO. I also welcome teaching of exercise and laboratory sessions, and I am committed to improving my Dutch for departmental teaching where needed.

Thank you for watching. I would be glad to discuss how my background can support a PhD topic with MeMC supervisors.
