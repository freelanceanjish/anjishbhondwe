### Job search sources (never rely on aggregators)

- **Never base job searches or recommendations on aggregator / reposter links.** This includes (non-exhaustive) Jobijoba, baan24, wearedevelopers, hirocareers, HubMub, trabajo.org, careerjet, ApplyKite, scholarshipdb, magnet.me, myscience, jobbnorge reposts, findjob24h, and similar.
- **Always verify each role on the employer's own careers page or official ATS** (e.g. Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Ashby, or a Talent-soft/Talentsoft page on the company's own domain) and confirm the posting is currently live before presenting it.
- For academic roles, the university's own vacancy page plus EURAXESS or AcademicTransfer are acceptable primary sources.
- **Only share a working, official application link.** If the official link cannot be confirmed live, say so instead of sharing an aggregator link.
- For every role presented, capture the **language requirement** and **location / relocation** constraint, since these are the most common filters.
